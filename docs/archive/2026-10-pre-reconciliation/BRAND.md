## 🎯 Purpose and Mission

**Project Slogan:**
> **"Everyone knows everything, and we decided to bring everyone together."**

This slogan is the heartbeat of OpenEdu Git. It reflects our belief that collective knowledge, when shared openly and collaboratively, creates the best educational resources for every student in Iran.

**Mission:** To build a living library of Iranian educational handouts where teachers can collaborate, students can find the best resources, and everyone can benefit freely and openly.

**Vision:** To become the primary reference for educational supplements in Iran, where quality is born from collective collaboration and educational equity is achieved.

---

## 🏛️ Preservation of Educational Knowledge

**Eternal Digital Archive**

- Handwritten handouts are converted into typed, precise digital versions with proper formulas and high‑quality rendering.
- All content remains accessible and preserved even after the author’s lifetime.
- Every version and edit is permanently stored and fully traceable, ensuring that teaching wisdom never fades.

**Knowledge Continuity**

- Teaching methods, unique explanations, and pedagogical insights are documented for future generations.
- The collective experience of Iranian teachers becomes a permanent national educational heritage.

## 💎 Core Values

1. **Freedom and Equal Access**
   - All students, regardless of geographic location or economic status, should have access to the best resources.
   - Educational content must always remain free and open.

2. **Transparency and Trust**
   - The change history of every handout is publicly visible.
   - Authors' names are always linked to their work.
   - Review and moderation processes are public and fair.

3. **Community‑Driven**
   - The best content emerges from collaboration and collective participation.
   - Teachers and students, as active community members, shape the platform's destiny.

4. **Respect for Intellectual Property**
   - The CC BY‑SA 4.0 license ensures that authors' names are always preserved.
   - No one may commercially exploit educational content without proper attribution.
   - **Intellectual Property Percentage:** Each contributor’s share is dynamically calculated based on the volume and significance of actual contributions (authorship, review, editing) and displayed on every handout.

5. **Innovation in Education**
   - Leveraging technology to improve the learning experience (online classes, interactive tests, AI assistant).
   - We are always improving and evolving based on the real needs of the community.

6. **Responsible Financial Sustainability**
   - Revenue model through commission on private classes and course sales, without restricting access to core content.
   - Revenue is used for development, support, and rewarding top contributors.

7. **🇮🇷 Persian‑First Identity**
   - The platform is built for Iranian educators and learners first.
   - Persian language and Iranian educational context are the primary considerations in all design and development decisions.
   - Right‑to‑left (RTL) layout is the default and primary orientation.

## 📊 Intellectual Property & Contribution Tracking

### Contribution Percentage Calculation

Each handout’s intellectual property is distributed dynamically based on real contributions:

- **Initial Author:** Baseline 100% ownership.
- **Editors/Contributors:** Ownership percentage is calculated using a weighted formula that considers:
  - **Volume of changes** – number of lines added/modified.
  - **Significance of changes** – major conceptual additions vs. minor fixes.
  - **Number of successful merge requests** accepted.
- The total always sums to 100%, and the calculation is transparent and auditable.

### Display Format

Every handout prominently shows:

- **Primary Author** – name with ownership percentage.
- **Contributors** – names with individual percentages.
- **Total** – always 100%.

**Example:**

```
📄 "Introduction to Derivatives"
👤 Ali Rezaei – 72%
✏️ Sara Mohammadi – 18%
✏️ Mehdi Karimi – 10%
```

This system ensures fair credit and motivates continuous improvement.

## 🎨 Visual Identity & Design Principles

The platform's design reflects its core values: transparency, community, and simplicity. Every visual decision is made with the end‑user in mind—teachers, students, and contributors.

### 1. GitHub‑Inspired UI Paradigm

- The user interface is **inspired by GitHub's familiar collaboration environment**, making it intuitive for anyone who has used a version‑control platform.
- Navigation patterns (top navbar, sidebar, tabs) and interaction flows (fork, pull request, merge) mimic GitHub's layout to reduce the learning curve.
- **Important:** The platform is **not** a skin or theme of GitHub. It is a fully custom‑built frontend (Next.js + Tailwind CSS) that adopts GitHub's usability patterns while maintaining its own identity and accessibility.

### 2. Brutalist Design Aesthetic

- **Content‑First Minimalism:** Decorative elements are kept to a minimum. Whitespace and clear hierarchy direct attention to the educational material.
- **Raw Typography:** Clean, legible sans‑serif fonts with bold headings and clear body text. The typography carries the style personality while optimising scanability and contrast.
- **Limited Colour Palette:** A fixed set of colours (see below) is used exclusively, primarily for interactive elements (buttons, links, badges) and status indicators.
- **Sharp Edges:** Boxes, cards, and buttons have straight edges (no rounded corners) to convey a straightforward, no‑nonsense attitude.
- **Grid‑Based Layout:** Components align to a strict vertical rhythm for a structured, orderly appearance. Use 4/8/12/16/24/32 spacing scale consistently.
- **Functional Animations:** Motion is reserved for user feedback—loading states, success/error transitions, and subtle hover effects—and is kept quick and purposeful (150–250ms).

### 3. Fixed Colour Palette

The definitive colour palette is established and documented below. **All colours in the frontend must be derived from this fixed set; no arbitrary hex values are allowed.**

| Role           | Hex       | Usage                                                                                          |
| -------------- | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary**    | `#DD614C` | CTA emphasis, primary actions, interaction signal, key interactive elements                    |
| **Secondary**  | `#DAA144` | Secondary actions, accents, supporting interactive elements                                    |
| **Surface**    | `#FFFFFF` | Large backgrounds, cards, content containers                                                   |
| **Text**       | `#111827` | Body copy, headings, all readable text — ensures legibility                                    |
| **Success**    | `#16A34A` | Success states, positive feedback, completed actions                                           |
| **Warning**    | `#D97706` | Warning states, attention‑needed notifications                                                 |
| **Danger**     | `#DC2626` | Error states, destructive actions, critical feedback                                           |
| **Neutral**    | `#FFFFFF` | Derived from surface token for official format compatibility                                   |

**Colour Usage Rules:**

- **Favour Primary (`#DD614C`)** for CTA emphasis and primary actions.
- **Use Surface (`#FFFFFF`)** for large backgrounds and cards.
- **Keep body copy on Text (`#111827`)** for legibility.
- **Do not introduce off‑palette colours** when an existing token can solve the problem.

### 4. Typography

| Property           | Value                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Primary Font**   | Darker Grotesque — for headings and body text                 |
| **Display Font**   | Darker Grotesque — for expressive headlines                   |
| **Mono Font**      | JetBrains Mono — for code, technical content, version history |
| **Weights**        | 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900                  |
| **Scale**          | Desktop‑first expressive scale                                |
| **Design Intent**  | Headings carry the style personality; body text optimises scanability and contrast |

**Typography Rules:**

- Use a clean, readable Persian font (e.g., Vazir or IranSans) for Persian text.
- Do not flatten hierarchy by using the same type size/weight for all text.
- RTL text should maintain proper letter spacing and alignment.

### 5. Spacing & Grid

- **Spacing scale:** 4px / 8px / 12px / 16px / 24px / 32px (based on 8px baseline)
- Keep vertical rhythm consistent across sections and components.
- Align columns and modules to a predictable grid; avoid ad‑hoc offsets.
- Use whitespace to separate concerns before adding borders or shadows.

### 6. Layout & Composition

- Prefer clear content blocks with consistent internal padding.
- Keep hierarchy obvious: headline → support text → primary action.
- Use whitespace to separate concerns before adding borders or shadows.
- **RTL First:** All layouts must be designed for RTL as the primary orientation, with LTR as the secondary fallback.

### 7. Styles as Variables

- All styling (colours, spacing, fonts, breakpoints) must be defined as **CSS custom properties (variables)** or within a Tailwind theme configuration.
- The design tokens are the single source of truth and must be referenced throughout the codebase.
- **No hard‑coded values** (e.g., `padding: 12px;`) are permitted in component styles—use the token system (e.g., `p-3` or `var(--spacing-3)`).
- This approach ensures global consistency, easy maintenance, and future‑proof theming (e.g., dark mode).

### 8. Prohibition of Inline Styles

- **Inline styles (the `style` attribute in HTML/JSX) are strictly prohibited**.
- All styles must be applied via CSS classes—using Tailwind utility classes, CSS Modules, or styled‑components with theme variables.
- Inline styles are not allowed because they bypass the design‑token system, hinder debugging, and make theming difficult.
- **Exception:** Dynamic styles that depend on runtime state (e.g., `style={{ width: progress + '%' }}`) are permitted only in such cases and must be clearly commented.

### 9. Multi‑Language & RTL/LTR Support

- The UI must fully support **Persian (RTL)** as primary and **English (LTR)** as secondary, with the ability to add more languages later.
- All text strings must be externalised using an i18n library (e.g., `react‑i18next`).
- Layout switching between RTL and LTR must be seamless, using CSS logical properties (e.g., `padding‑inline‑start`) instead of physical properties (`padding‑left`).
- Persian text should use a clean, readable font (e.g., Vazir or IranSans) and adhere to the brand voice guidelines: simple, educational, and avoiding unnecessary English loanwords.
- **RTL Default:** All layouts and components must render correctly in RTL by default. LTR support should be implemented as an alternative, not the primary design consideration.

### 10. Components & Interaction

**Components:**

- **Buttons:** Primary action uses `#DD614C`; secondary actions stay neutral.
- **Inputs:** Strong focus‑visible states, clear labels, and predictable error messaging.
- **Cards/sections:** Use consistent spacing and elevation strategy across the page. No rounded corners.

**Motion & Interaction:**

- Use subtle transitions that emphasise Primary (`#DD614C`) as the interaction signal.
- Default to short, purposeful transitions (150–250ms) with stable easing.
- Ensure hover, focus‑visible, active, disabled, and loading states are explicit.

## 🎯 Distinctive Features

1. **Git‑Based Collaboration Model**
   - Teachers can copy handouts, improve them, and propose changes for merging.
   - The complete change history of each handout is recorded.

2. **Quality Through Community**
   - A rating and feedback system helps teachers identify the best version of each handout.
   - Teachers can be categorised by their teaching style (conceptual, exam‑oriented, summary).

3. **Support for Multiple Formats**
   - Upload handouts in PDF, Word, Markdown, and LaTeX formats.
   - Automatic LaTeX‑to‑PDF conversion via CI/CD.

4. **Interactive Educational Tools**
   - Online test builder with auto‑grading.
   - Integrated online classes with whiteboard, file sharing, and session recording.
   - Interactive lessons with graphic and interactive elements (like Brilliant).

5. **Fair Economic Model**
   - Teachers earn income through private classes and course sales.
   - The platform takes a small commission as a service fee.
   - Core content always remains free.

6. **Persian Language Support**
   - Fully RTL user interface adapted for Persian.
   - Full‑text search engine with support for Persian letters.

7. **Knowledge Preservation**
   - All handouts are permanently stored and versioned.
   - Handwritten materials are digitised and converted to LaTeX for longevity and searchability.

8. **Transparent Teacher Credentials**
   - Dynamic, real‑time profiles based on actual contributions (authored content, successful merges, reviews).
   - No fake reviews or purchased points — all metrics are verifiable.

9. **Constructive Competition**
   - Specialised categories and rankings encourage continuous improvement.
   - Teachers are recognised for excellence in their specific expertise areas.

## ⚠️ Anti‑Patterns

- Do not introduce off‑palette colours when an existing token can solve the problem.
- Do not flatten hierarchy by using the same type size/weight for all text.
- Do not add decorative effects that reduce readability or accessibility.
- Do not mix unrelated visual metaphors in the same interface.
- Do not use inline styles — all styling must go through the design token system.
- Do not design for LTR first; RTL is the primary orientation.
- Do not use English placeholders in Persian content — use proper Persian text.
